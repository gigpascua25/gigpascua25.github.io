Projects

Key result

  1. Report automation at work

    Excel macros and rewritten process notes that cut the time spent preparing recurring UK pension reports at WTW, across 15–20 monthly and 5 quarterly reports.

    My day job. I designed and built the tools. Excel (VBA), with AI assistance. No code, data or client names published.

    5h → 1h preparation time for two recurring reports after I automated them

  2. Collections reporting pipeline

    An ELT pipeline that loads signed client contracts into PostgreSQL, validates them, and feeds a Power BI collections dashboard and an automated weekly email for a Collections Lead.

    Solo bootcamp capstone. Python, SQL (PostgreSQL on Supabase), Power BI.

    18 of 18 automated data-quality and reconciliation checks pass on every pipeline run

  3. LLM-assisted grant classification

    A Python pipeline that uses a large language model to suggest three ranked biodiversity targets for each conservation grant, each supported by a quotation verified against the grant text.

    Team of three. I wrote all of the Python code. Python, OpenAI API, pandas.

    88.79% of 339 human-tagged grants had the correct target among the model's top three suggestions (in-sample)

  4. Customer activity versus declared income

    An analysis for a bank's anti-money-laundering team that compares each customer's 2025 transactions with the income declared at account opening. Synthetic bootcamp dataset.

    Team of six. I wrote the executive summary and business context, and carried out this analysis, one of the team's five investigations. SQL (BigQuery), Python.

    ~120 customers moved more than their declared income in 2025 without triggering any alert

  5. Margin leakage analysis

    An analysis of 51,290 e-commerce order lines that tests five explanations for four years of flat profit margin and finds deep discounting most strongly associated with losses.

    Team of six. I investigated whether discounting explains the profit leakage and presented those findings. Spreadsheets.

    −$815K net loss on sales discounted by more than 20%, against total profit of $1.47M

  6. Discount governance dashboard

    A five-page Power BI report that evaluates whether deep discounts justify their cost and recommends where to pilot tighter discount controls.

    Team of six. I co-designed pages 2 and 3 with a teammate and contributed to the design of page 4. Power BI.

    92.2% of loss exposure came from the 15.3% of revenue discounted by more than 20%
