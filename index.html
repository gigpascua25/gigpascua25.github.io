Projects

The figure that mattered

  1. Collections pipeline for signed contracts

    An ELT pipeline that turns 12 signed statements of work into a Power BI collections dashboard and a weekly email for the Collections Lead.

    Solo capstone. Python, PostgreSQL on Supabase, Power BI.

    12 of 12 contracts set the first payment on the signing date, before the program had run

  2. Tagging conservation grants with an LLM

    A Python pipeline that reads each grant's objectives and returns three ranked biodiversity targets, each backed by a quote a reviewer can check.

    Team of three; I wrote all of the Python. OpenAI API, pandas.

    88.79% of 339 human-tagged grants had the right target in the model's shortlist of three

  3. Spending against declared income

    A check of whether bank customers' 2025 transactions match the income they declared, written for an anti-money-laundering team with more alerts than investigators.

    Team of six; I wrote the executive summary, business context and Investigation 4. SQL on BigQuery, Python.

    ~120 customers moved more than a year's declared income without ever raising an alert

  4. Where a profitable retailer leaks margin

    An analysis of 51,290 e-commerce sales lines that traces four years of flat margin to deep discounting.

    Team of six; I presented the discount findings. Spreadsheets.

    −$815K lost on sales discounted beyond 20%, against $1.47M of total profit

  5. Discount governance dashboard

    A five-page Power BI report that tests whether deep discounts earn their cost, and where to pilot tighter controls.

    Team of six; I designed two of its five pages. Power BI.

    92.2% of loss exposure came from the 15.3% of revenue discounted above 20%

About

I studied technical communication at Mapúa University, which is mostly the craft of making complicated things easy to follow. Since 2024 I have worked at WTW's Manila delivery center, producing reports on UK pension schemes: membership figures, service-level reports, data-cleaning checks and accounting extracts. I was promoted to Senior Associate in 2026. I also lead my team's process reviews and train new colleagues on the reports I know best.

That work taught me that a report is only useful if its numbers reconcile and its instructions can be followed right the first time.
