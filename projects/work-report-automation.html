Report automation at work

Excel macros and rewritten process notes that cut the time my team spends preparing recurring UK pension reports. This is my day job at WTW, so no code, data or client names are published.

Team
Special Projects team, UK pensions outsourcing, WTW Manila. April 2024 to present.
My contribution
I designed and built the tools on this page and rewrote the process notes. I also lead the process-documentation reviews for one of my team's two workstreams and train new colleagues.
AI assistance
I use AI tools to help write the VBA code. I design the steps, the checks and the outputs, and review and test every tool myself.
Tools
Excel (VBA macros, pivot tables, VLOOKUP, mail merge), the pension administration system, the document management system and JIRA.
Data
None published. The tools, data and reports belong to my employer and its clients.

Business context

My team produces statistics, KPI and accounting reports about UK pension administration for 77 schemes in three business groups. In a typical period I run 15–20 monthly reports, 5 quarterly reports and at least 5 weekly tasks, and report case volumes and SLA attainment to UK project team leads. One monthly SLA report alone covers more than 1,000 billable cases. Each week I also run a SQL query supplied by the UK team on case-completion data and refresh the dataset behind the operations team's Power BI dashboard.

Much of that time went on setting up working files by hand and on repeated manual checks. The question I kept asking was which of those steps a tool or a clearer set of instructions could take over.

What I built and changed

  • Report macros. Each macro takes the system extracts, sets up the working file and builds the final report, so the remaining time goes on review. The governance and membership-figures macros cut a five-hour job to about one hour, including the review.
  • Lookup and printing tools. A lookup tool works across several large files to set up the quarterly retirement case report and saves about two hours. A printing macro prints each query case and saves it with its reference number for upload, cutting handling from 45 to 30 minutes per member.
  • Process notes. I rewrote the notes for 9 of the 50 work types that drew the heaviest error feedback from UK reviewers. Since then, those reports have consistently received sign-off. I also wrote the notes for a newly onboarded membership-movement report, which cut its processing time from five hours to three.
  • Process-notes tracker. An Excel tracker lists every process note the team uses and flags those that need updating because of UK legislation changes, new processes, or errors.
  • Survey run guide. Colleagues kept making errors when filtering 2,000+ survey entries down to a final list of 800: missed exclusions, duplicates and case-code priorities. I rewrote and taught the run guide, and now everyone gets it right on the first try.
